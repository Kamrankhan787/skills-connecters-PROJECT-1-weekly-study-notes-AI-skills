## Formatted Output Expectation
```markdown
# Cellular Respiration: Lecture 4 Study Notes

## Glycolysis & Transition Reaction
- **Glycolysis**: Cytoplasmic anaerobic pathway breaking glucose into 2 pyruvate molecules, netting 2 ATP and 2 NADH.
- **Pyruvate Oxidation / Transition Reaction**: Mitochondrial reaction converting pyruvate into Acetyl-CoA while releasing CO2.

## Krebs Cycle & Oxidative Phosphorylation
- **Krebs Cycle (Citric Acid Cycle)**: Enzymatic cycle in the mitochondrial matrix producing 2 ATP, 6 NADH, and 2 FADH2 per glucose.
- **Electron Transport Chain (ETC)**: Inner membrane (cristae) proton-pumping series yielding 28-32 ATP via ATP synthase.
- **Terminal Electron Acceptor**: Molecular oxygen ($O_2$), which accepts electrons and hydrogen ions to form water ($H_2O$).
- **Fermentation**: Anaerobic pathway generating lactic acid or ethanol when oxygen is unavailable.

## Active Recall & Review Questions
1. Where within the cell does glycolysis take place, and why can it proceed in the absence of oxygen?
2. What are the net energy carrier yields (ATP, NADH, FADH2) produced specifically by the Krebs cycle per glucose molecule?
3. What role does molecular oxygen play at the end of the electron transport chain, and what occurs if it is absent?
```
